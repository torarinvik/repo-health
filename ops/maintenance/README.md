# Maintenance review records

Keep one dated record per scheduled or material-change review here. There are
no completed review records in this directory yet; the schedule in
`docs/operations/MAINTENANCE.md` is not evidence that any review was performed.

Name records `YYYY-MM-DD-<review>.md` and include:

- cadence or trigger and review scope;
- scheduled date, completion date, or explicit `not_performed`, `blocked`, or
  `retired` state with reason;
- primary and secondary operator roles, when assigned;
- evidence paths and the versions or digests reviewed;
- findings, impact, owner role, due date, and follow-up status;
- next due date.

Do not store secrets, personal contact details, or restricted pilot case data.
Keep the relevant access-controlled record in the operations system and link
only its non-sensitive identifier here when public evidence cannot be retained.
